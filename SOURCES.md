# INFORMATIVE PUBLIC SOURCES

Status: informative, not protocol authority.

Somavera rules come from the ratified capsule, not from external sites. These sources explain standards and legal questions that informed the draft.

## Technical

- RFC 8785, JSON Canonicalization Scheme: https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc8785/
- RFC 8785 verified errata: https://www.rfc-editor.org/errata/rfc8785
- W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model 2.0 history and Recommendation: https://www.w3.org/standards/history/vc-data-model/
- W3C Verifiable Credentials overview: https://www.w3.org/2025/credentials/
- CometBFT state sync: https://docs.cometbft.com/v0.37/core/state-sync
- CometBFT light-client specification: https://docs.cometbft.com/v0.38/spec/light-client/
- Cosmos SDK state/store concepts: https://docs.cosmos.network/sdk/latest/learn/concepts/store
- Cosmos SDK upgrade guidance: https://docs.cosmos.network/sdk/latest/guides/upgrades/upgrade

Referencing these projects does not commit Somavera to their software. A ratified activation manifest selects exact implementations and versions.

## United States legal and reporting starting points

- SEC, March 17 2026 Commission interpretation concerning federal securities laws and crypto assets: https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/interp/2026/33-11412.pdf
- SEC small-business crypto asset resources: https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/crypto-assets-federal-securities-laws
- FinCEN guidance on administrators and exchangers of convertible virtual currency: https://www.fincen.gov/resources/statutes-regulations/guidance/application-fincens-regulations-persons-administering
- IRS digital-asset filing information: https://www.irs.gov/filing/digital-assets
- IRS reminder on reporting digital-asset income and transactions: https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/report-digital-asset-income-including-cryptocurrency-on-your-tax-return

These sources are not legal or tax advice and do not determine VERA’s treatment. Classification depends on the actual code, offers, distribution, marketing, governance, and operator conduct in each jurisdiction.
